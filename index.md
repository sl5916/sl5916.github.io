## Projects

### Project 1: Exploring the Distribution of Diamond Prices

In this project, I analyze the prices of 53,940 diamonds to investigate how diamond prices are distributed and whether the mean accurately represents the price of a typical diamond.

I use pandas and the Python standard library to calculate the mean, median, and mode, and create a text-based bar chart to visualize the price distribution.
